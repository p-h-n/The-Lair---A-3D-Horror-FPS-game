THE LAIR

You are in a maze - one with no landmarks, no guidance and numerous dead ends. Somewhere within this labyrinth lies an exit, though reaching it will demand far more than navigation alone, for you are not the only presence here. These narrow corridors and dim yellow walls are home to a number of inhuman wardens. The Seekers. They do not eat. They do not rest. They exist with a singular purpose: to patrol the maze searching for a single heartbeat. Your heartbeat. The moment they hear it, they will know precisely where you are, and they will not stop until you are either beyond their senses or within their grasp. You were never meant to be here and they know it. Find the exit. Evade the Seekers. Do not die.
